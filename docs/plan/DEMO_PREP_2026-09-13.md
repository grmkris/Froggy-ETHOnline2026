## What Froggy is now

A wallet for your agents. You buy **credits** (100 = $1) with USDC on Base or HBAR through x402. The agent spends them on tools, research, a shared hosted browser, email and watchlists, inside limits it cannot change. External agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) connect over OAuth at `/mcp` and use the same balance.

## The agent's tools, by group

- **Research:** `web_search` 1 cr, `x_search` 6 cr, `inference` 1 cr (through `service_run`); `graph_discover`, `graph_schema`, `graph_read`, `graph_query`; `research_read` (Pinax, Polymarket, Hyperliquid, DefiLlama, Hedera). `brief` 5 cr over MCP, CLI or the task API.
- **Tokens:** `market_search`, `token_inspect`, `token_snapshot`, `token_research`, `rpc_read`, `quote_action`, `watch_launches`, `pons_token`.
- **Create:** `image` 8 cr, `speech` 10 cr, `email_document`.
- **Browse:** `browse_task` and the `browser_*` tools, only inside a paid browse.
- **Email, Watchlist, Reminders:** mailbox tools, watchlist and monitor tools, `track_wallet`, `onchain_alert_configure`, `schedule`, `notify`.
- **Trading:** `trade_prepare`, `trade_simulate`, `trade_execute`, `trade_status`, `positions` — paid from the wallet, not credits.
- **Pay:** `x402_fetch`, `x402_probe`, `wallet_send`, `wallet_status`, `credits_balance`.

Always asks a human: `x402_fetch` to a new URL, `wallet_send` (kind `transfer`), trades, email sends, dapp transactions and signatures. Never a tool: anything that raises a limit or approves a spend. Refused: an address that came from the model or a page.
