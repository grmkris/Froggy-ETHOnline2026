# Rail decision, 11 September: drop Hedera? move to Arc? (FABLE51)

Written Fri 11 Sep 2026, 16:30 CEST, for Jonas to answer Kristjan's "wdyt" on dropping Hedera, and to settle the Hedera / Arc / Base question before the internal cut (Sat 12 Sep 20:00 CEST, 27 hours away) and the submission (Sun 13 Sep 18:00 CEST). Built from three read-only audits of HEAD `5b3a93f` (the money rails, the Graph and Privy coverage, the chain configuration) and live reads today of ethglobal.com, docs.arc.io, developers.circle.com, docs.privy.io and the x402 repositories. Nothing in the tree was changed. Estimates are engineer-days for someone who knows this repo; probabilities are my judgement and are marked as such.

## The answer in six lines

1. **Do not change the rail before Sunday.** Keep Hedera working under the hood, nominate Privy + The Graph + Hedera, and change the copy so the product reads "the USDC wallet for your agents", with Hedera as the settlement line on the receipt. Half a day, no money-path change, no test or policy change.
2. **"Drop Hedera in one hour" does not exist.** Hedera is about 2,600 lines across `packages/payments`, `packages/wallet` and `apps/server`, plus the 1,700-line agent door, five user columns and a `conversions` table, four ADRs, and 409 of the repository's 932 tests touch its fixtures. Removing it is 4 to 5 days. And Froggy cannot accept an EVM payment for its own services today: the seller side has only the Blocky402 gate and a stub, so a Base-USDC seller is 1 to 2 more days.
3. **Arc for Sunday is 2 to 4 days of work for a worse bet.** It would replace the Hedera nomination (the rule is three partners) with a single-winner bounty we would enter with a testnet lane built in a day, against teams built on Circle's own wallets. The local prize sheet is also stale: Arc's bounties are now $3,500 each, with $2,500 of that paid only if the same project is live on Arc mainnet by 30 September, and there is no separate "push to mainnet" bounty any more.
4. **Arc is the right rail after Sunday if the team wants USDC-only with no gas wall.** USDC is the gas token, the USDC contract speaks EIP-3009, Circle Gateway is an x402 facilitator on Arc testnet, Privy supports any EVM chain, the installed viem already ships `arcTestnet`, and mainnet opens on 16 September. Jonas is right about the destination and wrong about the date; there is no prize for adding Arc after the submission, so that is a product decision, not a prize decision.
5. **The friction Jonas hit is Base ETH gas, not Hedera.** A person's USDC transfer on Base is broadcast from their own EOA and pays gas in ETH; there is no relayer, and Privy's sponsored-gas credits on this app are empty. Only the x402 EIP-3009 path is gas-free for the person. For the video: put HBAR straight into the demo person's Hedera account (address on the Wallet page) and the conversion never runs.
6. **The Graph track is covered** (about 6/10): one Messari standardized query across twelve lending deployments on four chains, freshness-gated, ranked into a decision, hashed onto receipts, resold as a paid brief, and a proven $0.01 x402 pay-per-query on Base mainnet. The track text says building "meaningfully on a standardized schema (for example the Messari Standardized Subgraphs)" qualifies on its own. Three cheap improvements are listed in section 6.

## 3. The four options, costed

**A. Keep the Hedera rail, change the copy. Half a day. Recommended for Sunday.** README lines 5, 9, 28 and 122-138, `x402-demo.ts`, `wallet-home.tsx` ("USDC + HBAR"), `service-task-card.tsx` ("Paid on Hedera"), `add-funds.tsx`, the wallet chip labels. The product becomes "a USDC wallet for your agents; one dollar balance; every receipt names the rail it settled on". Nothing on the money path moves, the 990 unit tests and 136 browser tests stay green, the Hedera nomination and its evidence stay valid. The video shows HBAR only on the receipt line.

**B. Drop Hedera, sell on Base USDC over x402. 5 to 7 days.** Removal (4 to 5) plus the Base seller (1 to 2). Not before Sunday under any split of Kristjan, Hemang and kermes. After Sunday it is the smallest EVM-only rail because the buyer half and the gate wire already exist. What it does not fix: the person's own transfers and trades still need ETH on Base (or Privy gas credits, which is a dashboard billing action by the owner). What it costs in prizes: the Hedera seat, for Uniswap or Bazantic at a lower ceiling.

**C. Add Arc. Additive lane 2 to 3 days; full move 3 to 5 days.** The additive lane: `arcTestnet` in Privy's `supportedChains`, an `eip155:5042002` entry across the roughly 18 files that hard-code Base (`packages/payments/src/evm.ts`, `packages/domain/src/money.ts`, `apps/server/src/{environment,services,purchases,service-providers,conversion}.ts`, the web explorer links and copy), a second person-policy rule per chain (Privy rules are per chain id), a Gateway `BatchEvmScheme` payer in `packages/payments` with a `tools/graph.ts` allowlist edit, the one-time Gateway deposit as an `eth_signTransaction` rule, ArcScan links on receipts, a stub, tests, an architecture diagram and a video segment. The full move adds the removal from option B. Neither fits before Saturday 20:00 without dropping the video, the top-up card and the bug triage. Two traps for whoever builds it later: native USDC is 18 decimals and the ERC-20 interface at `0x3600000000000000000000000000000000000000` is 6, so the native token must never be listed as "USDC" in `KNOWN_ASSETS` or `parQuote` misprices it by a trillion; and Arc silently drops transactions under 20 gwei `maxFeePerGas`.

**D. A simplified rebuild on Arc. Not possible by Sunday.** "Simplified" would mean a fresh, small Froggy: Privy wallet on Arc, a policy, one paid service, no Hedera, no conversion, no HBAR. That is a rewrite of the one thing the current tree does well (the leash, the receipts, the shared browser) in 27 hours, on a testnet, throwing away the mainnet evidence the submission already has. The simplification Jonas wants is real and cheap, but it lives in the copy and the onboarding (the top-up card when a paid action is refused, the one-dollar balance that already exists), not in the rail.

## 4. Arc, honestly

What is verified today (docs.arc.io, developers.circle.com, live RPC probes by the research agent):

- Testnet chain id 5042002, RPC `https://rpc.testnet.arc.io`, explorer `https://testnet.arcscan.app`, faucet `https://faucet.circle.com` (20 USDC per address every 2 hours). Mainnet: "Arc Public Mainnet will launch on September 16, 2026" (Arc blog, 5 Aug 2026); mainnet addresses "not yet available". The installed viem 2.56.3 ships `arcTestnet` and an `arc` stub with id 5042 and no RPC.
- USDC is the gas token; "any wallet that submits transactions on Arc must hold USDC". No paymaster is needed for an EOA. Native 18 decimals, ERC-20 interface 6 decimals, same asset. 0.5 s blocks, final on inclusion. Standard EVM tooling, EIP-1559, EIP-7702.
- The USDC contract implements EIP-3009 (`name` "USDC", `version` "2", the canonical `TRANSFER_WITH_AUTHORIZATION_TYPEHASH`). Arc publishes an EIP-3009 relayer guide. So the exact x402 scheme our buyer already signs works on Arc in principle; nobody public settles it there except Circle Gateway's batched variant.
- Circle Agent Stack = Circle CLI, Agent Wallets (Circle 2-of-2 MPC), Agent Nanopayments (Gateway), an Agent Marketplace (600+ x402 services, key-less discovery API) and Circle Skills. Starter kits are "intended for Arc testnet use only". Circle Wallets are not a qualification requirement of the bounty.
- Privy: any EVM chain via `viem/chains` or `defineChain` in `supportedChains`; `eth_signTypedData_v4` and `eth_signTransaction` on any chain id; policy `chain_id` is a static string compare with no enumerated list. Untested: a policy pinned to `5042002`, and Privy's server-side broadcast on that chain.
- Coinbase's CDP facilitator and x402.org do not list Arc. Circle's mainnet Gateway does not list Arc mainnet yet. So the $2,500 conditional part of the Arc bounty depends on Circle shipping mainnet and mainnet Gateway between 16 and 30 September, outside the team's control.

What "Froggy on Arc" would be, for the roadmap line in the submission: the person's Privy wallet on Arc holds USDC that is also its gas; the agent pays Froggy's services and Marketplace services over x402 (Gateway Nanopayments), under the same per-person Privy policy engine, with no cosmos-type wallet, no `raw_sign`, no USDC-to-HBAR conversion and no HBAR rate feed. Base and Robinhood stay for trading under the same signer, and App Kits' Unified Balance and Bridge (CCTP) give the one-balance story across Base and Arc. That is a genuinely simpler product than today's, and it is a post-hackathon project.

## 6. The Graph question ("is this bounty scope covered so far?")

Covered, with three cheap improvements. What exists: `packages/graph` runs one GraphQL document unchanged against twelve Messari standardized lending deployments (Aave v2 and v3, Compound v2 and v3, Spark, Euler on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon and BSC), decoded with Effect Schema, fanned out in parallel, with a two-hour freshness gate that fails closed. The agent ranks cheapest borrow and best supply across protocols (`graph_query`), the daily digest queries it unattended, the oracle resells it as a paid brief, and the evidence hash lands on the purchase receipt. The treasury has paid the Graph gateway 0.01 USDC on Base mainnet by x402 (Basescan `0x9355a0c0...`). The composable track's qualification says "either compose two or more of The Graph's products, or build meaningfully on a standardized schema (for example the Messari Standardized Subgraphs)", so the standardized half is met; the "AI Tooling or AI Use Case (From Scratch)" track names "let your agent pay per query autonomously with x402" and fits at least as well, and one nomination covers both.

Improvements in order of return: (1) a README table naming the query and the twelve deployment ids and chains, with one sentence on what the shared schema made possible, plus a fresh 12/12 evidence run in `docs/evidence/GRAPH.md` (the current table is 5-6 Sep and some docs still say "four"); (2) one person-wallet x402 Graph query on production recorded with its receipt, closing the `TODO(tx)` in `GRAPH.md:25`; (3) if the composable claim is wanted literally, the Subgraph MCP for discovery or a `skills/graph-lending/SKILL.md` with the registry as JSON, which the prize audit planned and the tree does not have.

Privy, for completeness: Best financial flow is about 6/10 (onramp, deposit addresses, agent-signed transfers and x402 payments all wired; the only mainnet-proven flow is the treasury paying The Graph; Earn rules exist but no deposit was ever made). Best B2B is about 3/10 with no organisation story; do not enter it.
