Written Thu 11 September 2026 against `7f786a9`. The question was whether the last two days of work — the agent door, Robinhood Chain in the trading lane, person-owned policies, Home and the three destinations, the design pack, the behaviour description — follow this repository's own patterns and read as clean code, and what to clean up before Saturday's submission versus after it.

## Verdict

**The code is in good shape.** Across `apps`, `packages`, `tools` and `e2e`: no `as any`, no `as unknown as`, no `@ts-ignore`, one justified `@ts-expect-error`, six justified next-line lint suppressions, no whole-file suppressions, no stray `console.log` outside the CLI and the spikes, no real TODOs, environment read only in entrypoints and configuration. CI runs the full `check`, the Postgres contracts and the browser suite on every push to `main`; the pre-push hook runs `check`. At `7f786a9` the type-aware lint, the typecheck and 990 unit tests are green.

**The recent lanes follow the patterns.** The door is careful, honest about what it verified, and tested by behaviour with injected ports rather than snapshots. The three trading commits extend the folder's shape rather than bolting on; `pons_token` is the best-argued of them. The payments changes are total functions returning sentinels, as decision 0005 asks.

**Three kinds of debt, in the order they matter this week.**

1. **Documents and copy contradicted the product.** The signed-out page promised a freeze the README says does not exist. Two live plan documents and the README described navigations replaced on 10 September and a browser removed on 9 September. `STATUS.md` stopped at 9 September. Two pasted web transcripts sat at the root, one tracked. All of this is fixed in the commits listed under "Landed" below.
2. **Two leash gaps are real at HEAD.** The action-kind authority table (`packages/domain/src/authority.ts`) is shown to the person and never consulted: the judgement built in `apps/server/src/session.ts` carries no `allowance`. And a cap change reaches Privy and the store but not the live mandate: `WorkspaceSession.applyAllowance` has no caller outside its tests. These are B-02 and B-03 in [the bug triage](../product-description/bug-triage.md). Wiring them is not a one-line change: the ask half of the table needs an `approval_threshold` rule that `defaultRules` writes only under `SPENDING_LIMITS`, which production has off, and the ceiling half would newly bind the just-in-time USDC-to-HBAR conversion at the person's default numbers. That is a change on the money path with no browser-test signal, two days before the video. It is recorded honestly in decision 0019, `authority.ts` and `person-policies.ts`, and wired after submission as one change.
3. **Duplication and placement.** The door re-declares the JSON-RPC transport that `mcp.ts` already has. The discovery documents landed as a two-hundred-line block inside `router.ts`, exported out of the router so a test can reach them; no other route module does that. Amount formatting exists four times, HashScan link builders three times, and the Pons network literal ten times against a constant that exists. The trading folder — 43 files, 17.7 thousand lines — has no `AGENTS.md`, and six of its files carry a header.
