Passbook and Lilypad

Two directions, one system. Every main screen of Froggy at phone and desktop width, rewritten as a consumer app that happens to run on rails the person never sees: no wallets, tokens, chains or hashes anywhere. Passbook is the light cut of the ledger idea, Lilypad the dark cut of the frog-as-agent idea. They share every component; only the variables differ.

My recommendation

Passbook. It is the light cut, it needs no dark inversion of the design system, and the consumer rewrite lands hardest on paper: a receipt book people already trust. Lilypad is the demo cut, and because it shares every component it can be added later as the phone theme without redesigning anything.

What the rewrite changed

Wallet became Balance with one switch that says whether Froggy can spend. Chains, addresses and hashes are gone from every screen. Settings regrouped into Reminders, Sites Froggy can pay, Security, Connected apps and Account. Agents connect with a link, not a token.
