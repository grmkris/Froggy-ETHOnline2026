# competition

## Summary

Verdict first: as of 4 Sep 2026, no shipped product occupies the exact cell "agent drives a browser the human can watch and grab + spend policy enforced outside the model + Telegram pager + EVM/Hedera + open source + consumer-facing". But the cell is being approached from three sides, and the "leash" half of it is now commodity.

The leash is table stakes. Every major wallet vendor shipped an agent wallet with caps/allowlists/approval-above-threshold this year: Coinbase Agentic Wallets (11 Feb, `npx awal`, MPC, session caps, x402, Base/Polygon/Solana; skills repo MIT, 126 stars), OKX Agentic Wallet (18 Mar, TEE, 50 sub-wallets, ~20 chains), Binance Agentic Wallet (Apr; x402 added 13 Jul; Agent OS 20 Aug with no-withdrawal sub-accounts), Privy agent wallets (delegated signing + policy engine: per-tx, rolling-window, recipient/contract/chain allowlists; x402 and MPP; free to 499 MAU), MetaMask Agent Wallet (GA 6 Aug: Guard/Beast mode, daily limits, protocol allowlists, Blockaid simulation, approval via Mobile/email with 5-minute timeout, $10k/month loss coverage; CLI only, no browser), Cloudflare Wallets (announced 4 Aug: Account Wallet -> per-agent Virtual Wallets with allowance/allowlist/max-tx; still reservation-only as of 19 Aug), TON Tech Agentic Wallets (28 Apr, Telegram-native, TON only), AWS Bedrock AgentCore Payments (preview 7 May, x402 with a CDP or Privy wallet, session spend limits), Lit Vincent (7,000+ agent wallets), Cobo (Pact model, 80+ chains), Crossmint/lobster.cash (virtual cards + Solana USDC, Mastercard deal), Locus (three-layer policy engine). ETHGlobal judges have also already seen the leash as a hackathon project repeatedly in 2026: HumanMandate (World humanId caps/revoke/Selfie step-up, Lisbon Jul 2026), Do Not Rug Me ("cap it, scope it, kill it", 0G), Hourglass (Safe revocable delegations), ENShell (policy shell vs prompt injection, Cannes). "Per-tx cap + daily cap + allowlist + kill switch" will not read as novel on its own.

The closest occupants of the browser+wallet cell are: (1) the OpenClaw skill `metamask-agent-wallet-skill` (author @andreolf, v0.1.0, MIT-0, ~3 months old) which drives a sandboxed MetaMask extension in an isolated Chrome profile with spend limits, chain allowlists and approval thresholds - but the guardrails live in a `permissions.json` checked by the skill itself (inside the agent process), and neither visibility nor human takeover is documented. (2) Cloudflare, which now owns both a HITL browser (Browser Run: Live View + Human in the Loop on 15 Apr; structured `Cloudflare.handoff`/`handoffComplete` CDP handoff on 28 Jul) and agent wallets with guardrails - as two separate infra products, not wired together, not consumer, not open source, and Wallets not GA. (3) Brave's BAT Roadmap 4.0 (9 Jul): browser-native x402 and MPP support, an open `bx402` experiment, and a unified Brave Wallet with prototypes "this fall"; it does not specify agent spend limits. Steel's MCP server explicitly frames "Take control" for "logins, CAPTCHAs, payments" and "Hand back" - the interaction grammar you are building is now standard in browser infra (Browserbase Live View read/write iframe, Steel `interactive=true`, Anchor, Auto Browser 781 stars noVNC, Vercel agent-browser 41.9k stars "pair browsing") but none of them carry a wallet or a policy.

Big-tech agent browsers are deliberately not in this market. OpenAI retired ChatGPT Instant Checkout on 24 Mar 2026 (fewer than 15 Shopify merchants ever went live; Walmart saw ~3x worse conversion) and moved checkout back to merchants. Claude Cowork's built-in browser (27 Aug) has "no access to logins for email, banking" and is positioned as public-info fetching. Chrome auto browse (Gemini 3, US preview for AI Pro/Ultra) pauses and asks for confirmation before any purchase. Perplexity Comet does commerce via PayPal Instant Buy in the user's own browser - visible and takeover-able, but no crypto policy. Opera Neon is $19.90/month with no wallet; Opera decommissioned its crypto wallet in Apr 2026. None of them will pay an x402 402 page or sign in a dapp; that is precisely the web your product targets.

Telegram: SingIt remains the only chat-native consumer competitor ("Spending limits your AI agent cannot break", Telegram/WhatsApp/iMessage, Base, x402, signed policy JSON). Its linked GitHub (bubon-ik/SingItAI) has 6 stars and describes an Algorand testnet + Firefly hardware-approval build, inconsistent with the Base product page, so real traction is unverified. Its tagline is nearly identical to your original "policy-bound spend" line; the browser must be in your tagline to separate.

Protocol layer: x402 governance moved to a Linux Foundation x402 Foundation (operational 14 Jul 2026, 40 members including Visa, Mastercard, Google, AWS, Stripe, MoonPay, Circle, Solana Foundation - Hedera is not a member); Chainalysis counts 100M+ x402 transactions through Q1 2026, mostly Base. MPP launched with Tempo mainnet on 18 Mar 2026; Privy supports it. Google AP2 v0.2 (28 Apr) added "Human Not Present" mandates, an x402 extension co-built with Coinbase, the Ethereum Foundation and MetaMask, and was donated to the FIDO Alliance. MoonPay PayBox (29 Jul) sits inside Claude/ChatGPT as a connector with passkey approval, permission levels like "under $100 or notify me", Solana + 7 EVM chains (incl. Tempo, Base, Robinhood Chain), x402 and Visa TAP - no browser, closed, no Hedera.

Two technical/positioning cautions from this pass. First, Bun docs state `Bun.WebView` is experimental and `headless: true` is the only implemented mode; the visible browser therefore must be a CDP screencast rendered in your web app, which is what ARCHITECTURE.md already assumes - verify `Page.startScreencast` works through the chrome backend's `cdp()` on day 0. Second, Blocky402 is described by Hedera as handling Hedera testnet V1 x402; confirm whether your hosted service can settle on Hedera mainnet or whether testnet is acceptable to the Hedera judges.

Honest white-space call: the empty cell is real but narrow. What is genuinely unoccupied and defensible in a 9-day window is the combination of (a) policy enforced by Privy outside both the model and the browser (contrast: OpenClaw skill's editable permissions.json), (b) a visible arbitration UX where the human grabs the same page, and (c) receipts that explain why a spend was allowed. Cloudflare could close the infra version of this cell within months; Brave could close the consumer-browser version by winter. Your moat for the hackathon and the first users is consumer + open source + Telegram pager + chain-agnostic (Hedera), and speed.
