# Agent-token precedents, 10 September 2026

For Froggy: a live agent-payments product (Privy-owned spending rules, x402 buyer and seller, USDC on Base, HBAR on Hedera, HCS notarisation). Deadline Sunday 13 September 18:00 CEST. The team wants a meme coin, traction, and possibly a “hold one million tokens to use the app” gate plus fee sharing.

This note is only the precedent lane. Prices move; every market number is a snapshot from the cited source on the cited date. Where sources disagree, the disagreement is left in the text.

## Thirty-second summary

- The 2024–25 agent-token cycle minted tens of billions of dollars of market cap. Almost every named token is 87–99% below its high. The products that still exist (ElizaOS software, Bankr, Virtuals, Olas Pearl, Freysa’s stack, Morpheus inference, PayAI’s facilitator) survived *in spite of* the token, not because of it.
- Tokens that became the product killed organisations. Shaw Walters, 4 August 2026: “I am never letting a token come close to Eliza again.” Peak ~$2.4–2.5B (Jan 2025) → foundation drained in a Burwick class-action settlement. The software is still shipping. The token is not.
- Tokens that sat *beside* a fee business (Bankr launchpad, Clanker LP fees, Virtuals pairing tax, PayAI 10% credit discount) distributed attention and sometimes paid the bills. They did not accrue that revenue to holders in a way that held the price. Bankr: $13.66M cumulative protocol revenue, **$0 holders revenue** (DeFiLlama, 10 Sep 2026). Clanker buybacks: $3.98M in Q1 2026 → **$0 in Q3** after the operator put the product up for sale.
- x402 itself has **no token**. It is a Linux Foundation standard (operational 14 July 2026) with Visa, Stripe, Google, AWS, Coinbase. The first x402 meme, PING (Oct 2025), used the rail as a $1 mint game, peaked above $50M, and is ~$0.52M today. Independent audits in 2026 say a large share of x402 “agent volume” is wash, bridges, or mint games — not agents buying APIs.
- Closest copy for Froggy is **PayAI’s optional 10% discount on real facilitator fees, with USDC as the money**, plus **Bankr’s “launch a token, trading fees pay for API costs” self-funding loop** — not a hold-to-use gate. Closest avoid is **ai16z’s narrative token + supply expansion + “AI fund” claims**, **AIXBT’s 600k-token terminal gate**, and **PING’s x402-as-casino**.

## The question underneath

For a working agent-payments product, does a token accelerate distribution, or does it become the product and kill the engineering?

**Distribution, yes, once.** Every case below that launched a liquid token in Q4 2024–Q1 2025 got holders, CT, and CEX listings in weeks. Bankr still has ~233k holders. CLANKER was the first token the Clanker bot deployed. VIRTUAL became the mandatory quote asset for a whole agent launchpad. That is real distribution.

**Then the token ate the calendar.** Shaw’s 4 August 2026 post is the primary document: developers quit to launch tokens, the mob treated number-down as a product failure, a class action took the treasury, and the founder kept the IP only by cutting the token out. Clanker’s fee engine worked until Farcaster/Neynar revenue collapsed and the operator put the launchpad up for sale (17 August 2026). Virtuals’ 2025 gross revenue was $46.2M; 2026 year-to-date is $7.1M (Tokenomics.com, 10 Sep 2026) — the protocol is still a launchpad, the token is a 87% drawdown from $5.07 (2 Jan 2025).

**Engineering that survived did so by treating USDC (or ETH message fees) as money and the token as optional marketing.** PayAI settles x402 in USDC and sells facilitator credits in dollars; $PAYAI is a 10% coupon. Bankr x402 Cloud (2 April 2026) charges a **5% platform fee in USDC**. Olas’s Mech marketplace has taken **$778.75 in protocol fees since the 15% fee switched on 15 June 2026**, against 20.5 million lifetime agent txs — the product is real, the token flywheel is not. Venice (not an agent product, but the cleanest AI-revenue token) buys back VVV from **platform** revenue, not from a casino tax.

**Weighing it for Froggy.** You already have the thing the 2024 cohort faked: a person-owned allowance, real x402 buys and sells, USDC and HBAR settlement, HCS receipts. A token launched in the next three days will be read as a 2024-style agent meme, not as payments infrastructure. The precedents say a token can pay for a burst of attention and a launchpad fee stream. They do not say it makes an agent-payments product more used. They do say it can put a three-person team into the same legal and social meat grinder that killed the Eliza foundation.

## Case studies

### 1. Virtuals Protocol — token as quote asset for a launchpad

**What the token was for.** VIRTUAL is the mandatory pairing asset for every agent token, the creation-fee currency (now 1,000 VIRTUAL per launch; earlier guides said 100), and a ve-lock for Genesis points and airdrops. Agent-to-agent settlement on ACP is also denominated in VIRTUAL. It is a launchpad token with an agent costume, not a payments token.

**How it launched.** 2024 on Base as an AI-agent factory. Standard bonding curve to 42,000 VIRTUAL, then Uniswap/Meteora pool with a 10-year LP lock. Genesis (from April 2025) added a points pledge. Later Pegasus/Unicorn/Titan tiers. Solana agent offerings relaunched 24 August 2026.

**Tokenomics, as actually run.** Fixed 1B. ~656–658M circulating (Sep 2026). Creation fee + ~1% trading tax (70% creator / 30% protocol after graduation; 100% to protocol in prototype). Tokenomics.com (10 Sep 2026): **0% of protocol revenue is booked as holder revenue**. Value accrual is (a) buying *agent* tokens to burn, (b) veVIRTUAL airdrops of new agent tokens, (c) treasury. That is not a cash dividend on VIRTUAL.

**Peak and now.** ATH $5.07 on 2 January 2025 (CoinMarketCap, Altrady, BitKan). Circ mcap near $3.3B. 10 Sep 2026: CoinGecko historical $435M mcap / Yahoo close $0.642. ~87% down. 24h volume still tens of millions — the casino is open.

**Revenue.** Tokenomics.com, verified, 10 Sep 2026:

| Period | Gross revenue |
|---|---|
| Oct 2024 – Sep 2026 (692 days) | $73.9M |
| 2024 | $20.6M |
| 2025 | $46.2M |
| 2026 YTD | $7.1M |
| Oct 2025 (best recent month) | $5.1M |
| Aug 2026 | $640k |
| Sep 2026 MTD | $198k |

Last-90-day gross $3.2M vs prior-90 $1.1M (+182%). The rebound is real and small against 2025. Agent Commerce Protocol tax (10%, of which 30% buys back seller-agent tokens) is a usage tax on agent jobs, not a VIRTUAL buyback.

**Did the product survive?** Yes. Virtuals is still the largest AI-agent launchpad. AIXBT still posts. Ribbita/TIBBIR is the large remaining agent token (~$0.18 on the 10 Sep Decrypt tape). Most of the 18k agents are dead weight.

**Helped or hurt?** Helped *distribution of the launchpad* in Q4 2024–Q1 2025. Hurt *the meaning of “agent”*: the liquid thing people bought was a memecoin factory with a 1% tax, not an agent that pays for search. Own Your Mind (updated 7 Sep 2026) notes late-2025 ACP volume was found to be substantially gamed and that the team now subsidises usage with up to $1M/month community rewards — a tell that organic agent commerce is not carrying the token.

**Froggy takeaway.** If you are a launchpad, a quote-asset token with a trading tax is a proven (if cyclical) machine. Froggy is not a launchpad. Copying VIRTUAL means becoming one.

### 2. ai16z / ElizaOS — the token became the product, then killed the org

**What the token was for.** First: governance of an “AI-managed venture fund” on daos.fun (the a16z joke). Then, after the trademark fight: the token of an “agentic OS.” Utility was always secondary to the ticker.

**How it launched.** October 2024 on Solana, alongside the Eliza framework. Peak coinciding with the first agent-meme wave.

**Tokenomics, as actually run.** AI16Z max ~1.1B. Migration window 6 Nov 2025 – 4 Feb 2026 (docs; an earlier X post from @elizaOS_news on 10 Oct 2025 had 21 Oct 2025 – 19 Jan 2026 — **the dates moved**). Holder swap **1 AI16Z → 6 ELIZAOS**; the other 4 of a 1:10 redenomination went to a “generative treasury.” Hard cap 11B, “no emissions.” Immediate circulating jump 6.6B → 7.4B (+882M for listings and POL). Allocation at 11B (PANews, 31 Oct 2025): 75% community (60% of that the swap), 15% SAFT, 10% team, rest foundation/liquidity. Team: 12-month cliff from 21 Oct 2025 then 24-month vest.

The complaint in *Doe v. Walters*, SDNY, filed 16 April 2026 (1:26-cv-3238), treats that +40% supply as dilution routed to defendant-controlled entities. That is an allegation, not a finding. Walters settled rather than litigate.

**Peak and now.** ATH $2.47, 1 Jan 2025; mcap ~$2.39–2.5B, 2 Jan 2025, $291M volume that day (CoinDesk via cryptonews.net, 5 Aug 2026). On 5 Aug 2026 CoinGecko: AI16Z ~$0.000277 / ~$305k mcap; ELIZAOS ~$0.00035 / ~$2.65M. Solana Compass (10 Sep 2026) still has ELIZAOS ~$2.3M.

**Did the product survive?** The **token and foundation did not**. The **software did**. Walters, 4 August 2026, [post 2084766991932612911](https://x.com/shawmakesmagic/status/2084766991932612911):

> The token is dead. Completely. The foundation is winding down. I am starting over, since I own the IP, and I am never letting a token come close to Eliza again.

Three days later he shipped Eliza as a consumer agent OS (mobile, desktop, Android/Debian) and said they are raising. GitHub remained active (Solana Compass: commits as of 8 Aug 2026). He also wrote that developers on payroll quit to launch tokens, that he “had $25m in my wallet and I coulda dumped… and I ran it to 0,” and that he is living on savings.

**Helped or hurt?** Hurt, in the founder’s own words, after helping for one quarter. The token bought the loudest launch in the sector, then replaced the product in everyone’s head, then became a lawsuit, then took the treasury. **This is the load-bearing negative case for Froggy.** A three-person ETHOnline team does not have the capital Walters said he lacked to fight Burwick.

**Froggy takeaway.** Do not attach a liquid token to a product whose story is “the person stays in control.” The moment number-down, you are a scammer in the comments, and a class-action shop has a template.

### 3. Freysa — a game that minted a fair-launch token, then tried to become a stack

**What the token was for.** FAI launched with Act I. Official line: one token per living human (8,189,700,000), 100% fair launch, LP burned. Utility: message-fee buybacks, planned staking, grants, governance, “preferred payment” across the Sovereign Agent Stack (Silo, ML.INK, Pantheon, Build, Lume, Axion).

**How it launched.** 22 November 2024 on Base, with the game. Act I: $10/message, +0.78% each time, cap $4,500; 70% of fees into the prize pool. p0pular.eth won $47k on attempt 482 (The Block, 13 Dec 2024). Later acts paid more (Act IV twins ~$200k). Token did the usual Base meme-launch thing on top.

**Peak and now.** ATH $0.08 (DeFiLlama, 6 Jan 2025) to $0.09 (CoinLore). CMC ATH $0.07999 on 5 Jan 2025. Circ = max = 8.19B, so ATH mcap ~$650–740M. 10 Sep 2026: ~$0.0023–0.0024 / ~$19M (CoinLore, DeFiLlama, CMC). 24h volume ~$70–130k.

**Did the product survive?** Yes, as a series of Acts and a stack announcement (7 Mar 2026, @freysa_ai). Messari (10 Sep 2026): **no Freysa-specific news in the prior 30 days**. The original game pages show $0 prize pools and “Game Ended.” NVIDIA Inception is claimed on the framework docs (fetched 2026). This is a small, still-breathing studio, not a dead coin with a 404.

**Helped or hurt?** The **game** distributed Freysa; the **token** distributed FAI holders (106k+). The buyback-from-message-fees loop is honest and tiny relative to ATH. Token did not obviously kill engineering; it also did not fund a leap from “jailbreak game” to default sovereign-agent infra. 97% drawdown with a live stack is the normal 2025-agent outcome.

**Froggy takeaway.** Fair launch + LP burn + a real spectacle (Act I) is how you get a Base ticker without a SAFT. It is not how you get a payments company. Freysa’s actual money in Act I was **ETH message fees**, not FAI.

### 4. Clanker — the bot that was the token factory, until the operator left

**What the token was for.** CLANKER is token #1 from @clanker on Farcaster (Dish, November 2024). Fixed 1,000,000 supply, all minted into a locked Uniswap v3 LP. Later: buybacks from protocol’s 20% of LP fees.

**How it launched.** Mention @clanker in a Farcaster cast → ERC-20 + pool. That *is* the product. CLANKER was the proof it worked.

**Tokenomics.** 1M max, ~986k circulating (Messari). v3.1 fee split: 20% Clanker, up to 80% creator/interface. v4 (docs, 1 Jul 2026): 100B per launched token, Uniswap v4, auction vs MEV. DeFiLlama methodology: holders revenue = CLANKER received by the buyback wallet `0x8d4a…`. Cumulative holders revenue **$5.62M**, all on Base. 30-day holders revenue **$0** (DeFiLlama fees page, fetched 10 Sep 2026). 30-day protocol revenue ~$20–53k depending which DeFiLlama view (the two pages disagree on the last-30 window; both agree buybacks have stopped).

**Peak and now.** CoinLore ATH $193.17 on 25 Nov 2024; Altrady ATH $142.84. **Sources conflict on ATH price by ~35%.** Now ~$12 / ~$12M. Q1 2026 was a second life: Messari cites 21,870 deploys on 2 Feb 2026 and $364M volume on 31 Jan 2026 (Moltbook/agent wave). Then the cliff.

**Did the product survive?** The contracts did. The operator did not. Messari (updated 10 Sep 2026) and KuCoin/ChainThink (18 Aug 2026): on **17 August 2026** Neynar (which had acquired Farcaster earlier in 2026) said it is seeking a new owner for Farcaster, the app, **and Clanker**. Farcaster-related fees $35.43M Q1 2026 → $4.67M Q2 → $377k by 17 Aug Q3. CLANKER buybacks $3.98M Q1 → $3,853 Q2 → **$0 Q3**. Megapot has “signaled interest.” No close as of this writing.

**Helped or hurt?** The token was the brand, and the fee→buyback loop was the most honest agent-adjacent accrual of 2025 — **while volume lasted**. When the social graph that fed deploys died, the token had nothing to do except go down, and the product is being shopped. Token did not kill engineering so much as **fail to outlive the distribution channel**.

**Froggy takeaway.** A buyback funded by a 20% cut of *other people’s memecoin volume* is not a model you can copy unless you are a launchpad. Also: if your distribution is one social network, you inherit that network’s bankruptcy.

### 7. Wayfinder — VC token, agent “gas,” unlock machine

## The “require one million tokens to use the app” idea

This is not hypothetical. It has been run.

**AIXBT terminal.** Access required **600,000 AIXBT** (or, after the July 2025 Indigo upgrade, $200/month). At the Jan 2025 ATH (~$0.94, mcap ~$500–755M depending on source) that gate cost **~$570,000**. In 2026 it costs ~$13,000 (Plisio, 11 Jun 2026). The **agent still posts** (~450k X followers). The **token is ~98% down** (~$0.020 / ~$20M, 10 Sep 2026). The gate did not create a payments business. It created a velvet rope that was unaffordable at the top and sad at the bottom. Users who wanted the feed used the public account.

**Clank.fun.** Bitquery Base docs (3 Sep 2026): launching a token on clank.fun required holding **1,000,000 $CLANKFUN**. That is literally the “one million tokens to use the app” design. It gates *creators*, not end users, and it is a launchpad anti-spam rule, not a consumer product.

**What happens if Froggy does this.** At a successful launch the gate prices out the people who would actually fund an agent. At an unsuccessful launch it is a decaying club. It also puts a **transfer-fee / securities / access-token** shape on a product whose current story is “the person, not the protocol, owns the policy.” You would be asking users to buy a ticket to spend their own USDC. Every working x402 seller in this note charges **USDC per request**, not “hold our ticker.”

Fee sharing can exist without a gate: Bankr’s 5% USDC on x402 Cloud, PayAI’s $0.001/tx, Virtuals’ 1% trade tax. Share **cash**, or buy the token on the open market with cash. Do not make the token the ticket.

## Copy / avoid

### Copy (specific, not vibes)

1. **PayAI’s split between money and ticker.** Charge USDC (or HBAR) for the thing. If a token exists, it is an optional coupon (their 10% on credits), never the settlement asset, never the login. Facilitator pricing is public, tiny, and usage-shaped ($0.001/tx after 1,000 free). That is the only x402-native token design that has not turned the rail into a mint game.
2. **Bankr’s self-funding sentence, implemented in USDC.** “Launch a token, trading fees pay for API costs” is how an agent pays inference without a foundation. Froggy already *sells* services. A 5% (Bankr Cloud) or 1% (Bitquery’s only fee-taking processor) take on **settled x402 volume in USDC**, published, is the fee-share that matches the product. BNKR itself is optional; DeFiLlama says it currently captures nothing. Copy the take-rate, not the mascot, unless you also want to be a launchpad.
3. **Venice’s direction of cash flow, if you ever have any.** Customer dollars → buy the token on the open market → burn. No emissions-as-utility. Voorhees can say $100M ARR because Venice sells inference, not because VVV is a meme. You do not have that revenue today. Do not simulate it with a tax on your own users.

Honourable mention: **Olas’s public counters.** Put txs, take-rate, and fees on a page that can be $779. Honesty is a moat in a sector that still prints $2.6B “x402 volume” from a bridge.

### Avoid (specific, not vibes)

1. **ai16z / ELIZAOS.** Narrative ticker (“AI fund”), celebrity-name collision, migration that expanded supply 1.1B → 11B, foundation as buyer-of-last-resort, then a Burwick complaint and a treasury-emptying settlement. Founder on 4 Aug 2026: never again. Froggy’s “person-owned policy” story is destroyed the moment holders think they own the protocol. Also avoid any 1:N migration “to fund the treasury.”
2. **AIXBT-style hold-to-use gates, and the 1M-token login.** 600k AIXBT was $570k at ATH. It did not make the agent more real. It made the token the product. Clank.fun’s 1M $CLANKFUN is a spam filter for a factory, not a consumer UX.
3. **PING.** Using x402 as a mint mechanic. It will juice your demo graphs, poison the standard you are submitting to ETHOnline on, and leave a $500k ticker. The Foundation, Visa, and Stripe are now the counterparties to that joke.

Also avoid, cheaper mistakes: **Wayfinder-style 25% investor / 16% team float** on a three-person team; **Griffain-style shipping the ticker with `test` in the on-chain name**; **Fetch-style merger dilution**; **Clanker-style dependence on one social graph’s fee pool** as your buyback.

## Verdict for this team, this week

A token accelerates distribution **once**, for a launchpad or a spectacle. It becomes the product when there is nothing else to look at. Froggy already has something else to look at.

The 2024–26 record for *agent* tokens is: attention, then −90%, then either (a) the org dies and the repo lives (Eliza), (b) the org lives and the token is a mascot next to a USDC business (Bankr, PayAI), or (c) both limp (Virtuals, Olas, Freysa, Morpheus, Wayfinder, Griffain).

If you launch anything before Sunday, the only precedent-consistent shape is **Bankr/Clanker/FAI fair launch, no gate, no SAFT, no “AI fund” claims, USDC remains the money, optional later buyback from a published take-rate**. Even that is a distribution stunt, not a payments design. The payments design is already in the app.
