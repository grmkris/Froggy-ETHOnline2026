## Summary

ETHOnline 2026 is an async ETHGlobal hackathon, 4-16 Sep 2026, 11 partners, $80k board (Graph $15k, Hedera $15k, Arc $10k, World $7k, 1inch $7k, ENS/Uniswap/Ledger/Privy $5k each, Chainlink/Bazantic $3k). I pulled the official rules page, the start page, the code of conduct, the partner pages, the event's embedded schedule JSON, and ~25 past showcase pages. Web search budget was exhausted before this task started, so everything here is from direct fetches (VERIFIED) or reasoning (INFERRED); X/Twitter could not be read.

Timeline (VERIFIED from the event page JSON, converted UTC-4): Kickoff + "Hacking Begins" Fri 4 Sep 12:00 EDT. Project Check-in #1 due Mon 7 Sep 23:59 EDT. Project Feedback Session #1 Tue 8 Sep 14:00 EDT; second feedback session Thu 10 Sep 09:00 EDT. Check-in #2 due Thu 10 Sep 23:59 EDT. Submissions due Sun 13 Sep 12:00 EDT (hard; upload of a non-conforming video fails). Judging Round 1 (asynchronous) starts Sun 13 Sep 15:00 EDT; Round 2 (live) Mon 14 Sep 12:00 EDT; Finale Wed 16 Sep 12:00 EDT. Only one sponsor-relevant workshop existed as of 4 Sep: Hedera's "Claude Code: AI Skills for Hackathon Builders" by Luke Forrest (3 Sep, YouTube -nkd3aorELM). No Graph or Privy workshop, office hours, or ETHOnline blog post was found on the schedule, partner pages, or their blogs. Named ETHGlobal judges in the JSON: Emilio Silva (Independent, @abcdemilio) and Karim Halabi (NGMI Labs, @0xkarim); Jake Russell (District) and Julio Cruz (PerkOS, who won Graph Continuity 1st at Lisbon 2026 with EQLTY) appear without a role.

Judging process (VERIFIED, ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/info/details): five criteria (Technicality, Originality, Practicality, Usability, WOW). Two rounds: async screening, then live judging for "typically only the top 20%"; live slot is 7 minutes (4 demo + 3 Q&A) with stock questions ("What inspired your project?", "What tools did you use, and why?", "What challenges did you solve?"). Crucially, partners judge their own prizes asynchronously, partners never see Round-1 results, and "the majority of prizes are paid out to projects that do not advance to the live judging." You pick up to 3 Partner Prizes on the last submission step; a partner with several tracks counts as 1, and for each you must write how you used the tool plus feedback. Privy (2 tracks) + Graph (2 fresh tracks) + Hedera (1 track) fits exactly.

Submission hard rules (VERIFIED): 2-4 minute video, >=720p, no speed-up, no AI voiceover/TTS, no music-with-text instead of narration, no phone recording (violations force a re-submit); repo with real commit history ("large single commits or missing histories may be disqualified"); AI tool usage must be attributed in the submission, and if you use spec-driven workflows "you must include all spec files, prompts, and planning artifacts in your submission repository". Team size up to 5, each member stakes ETH individually; stake refunded ~3 weeks after only if you submit before the deadline. Start Fresh: "all work on your project must begin after the hackathon officially starts"; public libraries and starter kits are fine, "project-specific code, designs, or assets" are not; undisclosed prior work = disqualification, prize revocation, possible ban. Continuity tracks require documenting pre-existing work in writing; Hedera's Continuity track additionally requires the project to have previously existed on Hedera, so it is irrelevant here; Graph offers a separate $5k Continuity AI pool. Note Hedera's track text says "settled through the Blocky402 facilitator" while Hedera's own x402 skill documents a self-hosted facilitator: do not self-host, use Blocky402.

What wins (VERIFIED showcase pages): Hedera "AI & Agentic Payments" at Lisbon 2026 went to Glassbox402 (also an ETHGlobal Finalist): x402ify any API, Hedera settlement via blocky402, HCS receipts, MCP server so Claude/GPT can pay, World ID human-vs-agent pricing, a real dashboard, 37 commits, README with problem/solution/how-it-works/run-locally/deploy. Kinora (A2A agents paying x402 through blocky402 with HCS-14 identity) listed no prize, so blocky402 + agents alone was not enough. The Graph's Lisbon write-up praised provenance and standardization: deeptrace ("responses are rejected unless the pinned deployment ID matches"), EQLTY ("risk gate only advances trade when evidence carries live provenance"), Am I Cooked ("one query format covers Aave v3, Compound v3, and Spark"), atlas (Standardized Subgraphs + Substreams + x402, won Graph 2nd and ENS). Graph explicitly said standardized schemas were "the biggest force multiplier." Agent-allowance projects without a distinctive angle did not place: Do Not Rug Me ("cap it, scope it, kill it", Lisbon 2026) and AgentPass (daily cap + Ledger approval, Cannes 2026) show no prize, while Maki ("keys locked in hardware, unreachable by the model") was a Cannes 2026 Finalist. This validates the shared-browser angle as the differentiator, and the leash as table stakes.

Hedera ships its own scoring rubric in hedera-dev/hedera-skills (hackathon-helper): Execution 20%, Success (Hedera metrics) 20%, Integration 15%, Validation ("what does the market think", real user feedback) 15%, Innovation 10, Feasibility 10, Pitch 10. Whether ETHOnline partner judges apply it verbatim is INFERRED, but it means real testers from the build-in-public push are literally score-bearing.

Base rate (INFERRED from ETHOnline 2025 showcase pagination): >=9 pages of ~35 projects (~280+), with roughly 2-4 prize badges per page, so about 8-10% of showcased projects carry any partner prize; top 20% reach live judging. The team's spec writer appears to have prior ETHGlobal form: SessionFlow (Cannes, Avail 2nd) links to github.com/grmkris/eth-global-prague-2025-mivio (handle match, INFERRED).

Showcase page fields (VERIFIED): title, tagline, description, "How it's made", embedded video, Live Demo URL, Source Code URL, prize/finalist badges, event. Pages are public and browsable by event/partner/category, so a live demo URL that keeps working after judging is the cheapest lasting visibility asset.
