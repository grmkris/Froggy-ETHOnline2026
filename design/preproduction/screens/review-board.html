1 · The mark, at real sizes

Below 40px the jaw shading and nostrils are dropped rather than scaled, so the silhouette and the eyes carry the identity. 16px is the favicon case: it still reads as a frog, which the current 🐸 emoji favicon cannot promise on every platform.

8 · What this board is not

Not live data, not a provider relationship, not advice, and not a claim that any payment route works today. Prices, wallets, merchants and timings are invented for review; the merchant domain uses .example on purpose. Readiness of each real integration is tracked separately in SERVICES.md, where a status is only ever as strong as its evidence.
