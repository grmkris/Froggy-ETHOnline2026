4 · The same shell, other task kinds

Travel

Result body becomes an itinerary with options; one price is watched on a stated cadence with an expiry. "Checked twice a day until 20 Sep" — never "monitoring".

Token research

Result body becomes evidence and risk findings with the chain and contract shown, each claim sourced and timed. A proposed trade is a review, not an action, and no screen claims a token is safe.

Paid service via MCP

The task names the assistant that asked and the allowance it was granted. The allowance is never wider than yours, and no tool can raise it.

5 · What this is not

Invented data throughout; merchant domains use .example. Not live provider evidence, not advice, and not a claim that card checkout works today — it is a human handoff, recorded as such in SERVICES.md.
